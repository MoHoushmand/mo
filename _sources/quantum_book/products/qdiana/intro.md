# QDiana: Quantum Education & ESG

> Quantum computing education platform with ESG certification and sustainability tracking. Making quantum literacy accessible to enterprises, universities, and governments.

QDiana addresses two interconnected challenges: the global shortage of quantum-literate professionals and the need to measure and reduce the environmental footprint of quantum computing infrastructure.

## The Quantum Skills Gap

The quantum computing industry is projected to require 300,000+ quantum-trained professionals by 2030. Current university programs produce fewer than 5,000 annually. QDiana bridges this gap through structured, competency-based certification.
