# Data Science

```{div} mo-lead
Statistics for data science, worked through in Python.
```

The [Statistics and Machine Learning](statML.ipynb) notebook covers the groundwork I use in data-science
projects:

- **Representing and visualising data**: line plots, pie charts, histograms, box-and-whisker, bar,
  violin and QQ plots.
- **Descriptive statistics**: central tendency, dispersion, the inter-quartile range, histogram bins
  and entropy.
- **Normalisation and outliers**: z-scores, trimming, Euclidean distance and min-max scaling.
- **Probability**: sampling variability, probability mass functions, the law of large numbers,
  conditional probability and the central limit theorem.

The notebook is shown with its stored results.
